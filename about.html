About Us

We providing best budget quality photography and Edit service since 2006.

I am Debashish Chakraborty, the founder of Studio Digital Eye. A love for painting during my student life may have helped me to establish myself in life as a photographer. After graduating in commerce in 2003, I finally learned multimedia and then started my career as a photographer and editor in a company. After working there for about a year, Studio Digital Eye was established in 2006 by me. Since 2006 Studio digital eye and my team members are serving quality photo shoot and edit for all our clients . Hope we can provide good quality service to all without any complaints like in the past.
